Keep aria-hidden, no Tab stop or click action.

Preserve .daily-scene-art/images pointer-events:none and original layering so fields remain fully usable. Prefer mouse-only delegated pointer tracking on the daily canvas and a bounds check for the visible illustration, excluding events over interactive/content controls. The art currently uses contain:paint, so remove/adjust only its local clipping as needed for outgoing particles while daily canvas still contains horizontal decoration. Use CSS end-opacity/entry class with no perpetual timers where possible. Clear/reset on prompt/route departure, respect current saved/system reduced-motion and coarse/touch pointers. No animation from typing, date browsing or clicks on form controls, and no record writes from this effect.

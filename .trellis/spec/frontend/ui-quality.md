# UI Quality

`shendu/public/styles.css` defines surface, ink, line, accent, radius, and shadow variables in `:root`. `applyTheme()` in `app.js` sets three accent variables and computes their text contrast. Theme settings expose six presets, custom colors, and a reduce-motion control. Shared UI must remain legible with every preset and custom accent.

The current shell has a shared top navigation and main column. Its mobile menu replaces the desktop links at narrow widths. The stage/calendar/pending-action content opens as a centered dialog from the top navigation, reusing the existing rail node. Narrow widths reflow the six overview cards, record reel, and existing forms. Check breakpoint transitions, not only wide desktop (`styles.css`).

- Preserve semantic buttons, form labels or accessible names, visible `:focus-visible`, and status feedback. `calendarHtml()`, the header menu triggers, and `openModal()` in `app.js` provide local examples.
- Dialogs use `#modal-root`; `openModal()` and the document key handler manage focus and Escape. Reuse that path for confirmation and settings dialogs.
- Keep both `@media(prefers-reduced-motion:reduce)` and the saved `reduceMotion` setting.
- The six overview category entries use local colored SVG illustrations in `assets/overview/`, with visible 日/周/月/季/年/决 marks and accessible category names. Asset provenance, source IDs and exact palette mapping live in that folder's README. Sizes are 112/96/84px at desktop/<=1280px/<=540px, derived from `--type-size`; <=1280px uses three columns. Keep each `.overview-type-card` fixed in the layout. Mouse pointer entry or keyboard-visible focus on its `.overview-type-trigger` opens `.is-expanded` and updates `aria-expanded`, scales only the internal mark to 1.14 with stronger shadow, and opens an independent `.overview-type-info` group with its name relation intact. Keep the icon upright; do not restore the tumble layer or rotate/move the hit button. Category introductions contain the name/purpose header, description paragraph and a footer with estimated duration and its enter button. Keep the paragraph at `margin:8px 0 0` so its default margins do not disturb the footer. The panel unfolds to the left at 240px wide, becomes 220px at <=1280px, and opens below with column-edge alignment at <=960px; never increase the container width or move the other five entries. Both the icon trigger and the panel enter button use shared `data-nav` for direct mouse, keyboard and touch navigation. Do not nest buttons or add a second click binding after shell navigation. Keep one open entry. Mouse departure closes it unless keyboard-visible focus remains inside; focus departure closes it unless the pointer remains inside. A transparent hit bridge supports moving across the gap to the enter button. Touch ignores pointer entry/departure. Escape restores focus before closing expanded states so focusin cannot reopen the dismissed panel. Use shared setOverviewType()/closeOverviewTypes(). Verify all six panel bounds, footer/enter visibility, direct navigation from both controls and unchanged sibling geometry, including 961/1280/1281px transitions. Reduced motion must preserve immediate access to the information.
- Header branding reuses the original red `.seal` and serif `慎獨` wordmark, without rotation or theme recoloring. Top-level header links, menu/rail/account/mobile triggers have no frame, fill, capsule shadow or active dot. Current text uses its route/category color; retain separate `:focus-visible` outlines. Navigation dropdowns use `setHeaderMenu()` for a single open menu; support arrow entry/movement, Home/End, native Tab, focus-out dismissal and Escape focus restoration. The review popup has two columns but remains a disclosure of navigation buttons, not an ARIA application menu; its internal grouped surfaces may retain borders and fill. Its animation and header transitions must respect both reduced-motion settings.
- For translucent surfaces, make text, controls, focus rings, and status labels readable over all supported backgrounds and themes. Establish hierarchy with grouping, type, and spacing before effects.
- The top stage/calendar button opens the existing `#right-rail` as a centered dialog at every logged-in route, without duplicating calendar IDs or event bindings. `.right-rail.open` uses a 900px maximum width and internal scrolling; <=680px switches the stage/calendar/pending cards to one column. Its title/close header appears only in the open state; on every route the closed rail is hidden, inert and absent from layout, so no review or other page retains a persistent side column. `setRailOverlayOpen()` controls dialog naming, main/header inert state, body scroll lock and `#rail-backdrop`, keeps Tab inside, and restores focus to `#rail-toggle` on Escape/close/backdrop dismissal. Calendar navigation closes the parent before the unsaved-change guard and route change. Existing `openModal()` child dialogs sit above the rail, make the parent inert, then restore parent interactivity and the invoking control on close. Keep backdrop/parent/child levels at 85/90/110 and clean open state before shell replacement. Verify month switching, date/record entry, stage editing, keyboard containment, close/backdrop paths and narrow/short viewports.
- The overview record reel moves continuously along a shallow U-shaped arc (sides high, center low), with card angles following the tangent. Do not substitute a straight translation with alternating card tilts. Cards are 276px high, `clamp(260px,22vw,292px)` wide on desktop and 240px at <=540px. Keep the category/status row, date/title, excerpt and footer in the same semantic order in both `record-layout-story` and `record-layout-note`: story places its illustration beside the title and uses a two-line full-width excerpt; note uses a full-width title, lower-corner illustration and three-line excerpt with reserved image space. Titles clamp to two lines; footer space is fixed so long text cannot push the entry out of the card. Status labels must reflect actual `STATUS`. Art comes from the locally hosted authorized `assets/reel-emotions/` collection, selected from the complete15-image pool through local explicit keyword matching, with random unused-image preference on no match and best-theme choice on ties. Allocate once per render so loop copies match; new overview visits may change fallback art. Layout remains stable by ID except date-only titles use the full-width note layout. Do not select by array index. Decorative art is `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events:none` and has reserved dimensions; provenance belongs in that asset directory's README. Keep the moving button's hit geometry stable: its internal `.overview-record-surface` provides the stronger hover/focus lift (-14px), scale (1.055), shadow and category border; only the footer arrow moves 5px right. Moving the button itself can lose edge hover and cause pause/resume oscillation. The visible pause control was removed by user request; only hovering an actual `.overview-record-card` pauses all cards. Use `.overview-reel-window:has(.overview-record-card:hover)`, never `.overview-reel-window:hover`: the window includes empty space below the category entries, which must keep moving. Keyboard focus exposes a static scrollable list; the saved `reduceMotion` setting and system `prefers-reduced-motion` preference disable motion and added transitions. Duplicated loop cards stay out of the accessibility and keyboard order. `bindOverviewArc()` measures the actual cards and recomputes the path on resize; `shell()` disconnects its observer before replacing the page. Loop resets and surplus record spacing stay outside the viewport. Regression-check both layouts with short/long dates, titles and excerpts at desktop/mobile; verify image/footer bounds, duplicate appearance, blank space (running), a real card including its lower edge (stable paused and raised surface), and blank space again (running).
- The daily metric-status `<select>` and date fields are intentionally native controls; their browser-owned popup geometry is acceptable in this self-hosted form. Keep their accessible names and test the opened controls at narrow widths when changing them.

- Daily review begins with a large centered Chinese paragraph and its start/continue/view button. A per-glyph typewriter preserves complete-text layout, exposes one full semantic copy to readers, lets start be activated immediately, and shows full text for either saved or system reduced-motion preferences. Cancel timers on intro departure or shell replacement.
- Writing hides/inerts the shared header and removes its reserved space only for daily. Show one question at a time: sleep, energy, each metric, risk, today, cause, action, obstacle, response, date, journal. One metric's action/status belong to one question. Use a small current group/question count, previous/next, manual save and an unsaved-aware exit. Completion only appears at the end; all other questions stay hidden and outside focus order. Every field needs a name; normal transition focuses the current question heading, error focuses its field. Returning to intro/other route and reauthentication must restore the correct shell state. Retain read-only verification/pending entry. The stage/calendar dialog alone contains the compact three statistics.

- Daily add/remove redraw restores focus to the added or nearest retained answer. New daily/stat text uses scoped `--daily-accent`/`--stat-text` (30% theme accent plus 70% ink), so white custom accents remain readable on pale surfaces; do not directly use arbitrary accent colors for small text. Lock confirmation carries `data-review-lock-busy`/`aria-busy` while writing, blocks dismissal and route departure, then restores retry/dismiss on failure. Verify both a delayed save and a failed/retried completion.

`npm run check` is the existing browser-script syntax gate. The test suite covers security and backups rather than visual behavior (`shendu/package.json`, `shendu/tests/backup-security.test.mjs`). Inspect actual desktop/mobile states and exercise affected keyboard and pointer paths for UI changes.

The icon stays upright with only its existing scale/shadow feedback; do not rotate it or animate the hit button/category/sibling geometry. Allow re-entry replay without a continuous loop. For both body.reduce-motion and system prefers-reduced-motion, hide `.overview-type-burst` while keeping introduction content and direct navigation usable. No external player/network dependency.

- Day-based reel cards show the date and full weekday as separate block lines. Date-only cards use the full-width note layout; on note cards with a custom title, reserve 88px for the two date lines plus title and constrain the illustration to avoid overlapping long titles. Verify 390px long-title geometry as well as non-day period labels.

- Reel path bounds must include all visibly intersecting rotated corners in normal and hover states, including lift/scale from the shared CSS variables. Derive top inset and total height from these bounds rather than a fixed center-point y value; reserve shadow space. Keep horizontal clipping and check full-cycle positions, both side edges, and hover on desktop/narrow widths. A center-point path inside a container does not mean its card edges are inside.

- The verified reel uses all deduplicated records, with no 12-record cap. Render one accessible original set, then fill the measured arc using whole copies of that set; `overviewLoopMetrics()` ensures slot count is a multiple of the original count and total path length equals slots × (card width + normal gap). Never pad just a partial prefix of the next round. Since motion runs from offset-distance 100% to 0%, the delay must advance logical record order at the fixed viewport entry (currently duration × (index/count − 1)); checking only simultaneous positions will miss a reversed or incomplete sequence. On resize, add/remove complete clone groups and synchronize their clocks; delegate card clicks at the track so new copies navigate correctly. Verify 1/2/5/7/13/30 unique records through two whole logical rounds, normal spacing at the loop seam and visible coverage across desktop/mobile, including resize. No verified records must show a verification-specific empty state and action-verification entry.

The main top padding is 22px desktop / 12px at <=820px. Keyboard-focus and reduced-motion static reels remove the negative margin and reserve extra top padding for lift. Verify all six trigger corners using actual hit testing, expand/click their information controls, and check normal/hover card bounds and header layout on 320/390/820/960/961/1280/1660px.
